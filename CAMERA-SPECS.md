# FilmBoard Camera Profiles

FilmBoard uses the selected camera's active sensor area with the project aspect ratio and focal length to calculate Camera View field of view. The full sensor and default recording area are kept separately when the manufacturer publishes both. Bit depth describes the highest manufacturer-documented recording or RAW-output path; internal and external paths are identified where they differ.
